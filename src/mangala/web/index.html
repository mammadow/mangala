📖 How to Play Mangala

Authentic Turkish rules for 48 stones across 12 pits & 2 treasuries.

🌰 1. Sowing Rule

Pick up all stones from one of your pits. If it has > 1 stone, leave 1 stone behind and sow the rest counterclockwise one by one. If it has only 1 stone, pick it up and place it in the next pit. The opponent's treasury is always skipped.

⚡ 2. Extra Turn

If your last sown stone lands in your own treasury (Hazine), you earn an immediate bonus turn!

🎯 3. Even Capture

If your last stone lands in an opponent's pit and makes that pit's total count even (2, 4, 6...), you capture all stones from that pit into your treasury.

🔄 4. Empty-Hole Capture

If your last stone lands in your own previously empty pit (making it 1 stone), and the opposite opponent pit contains stones, you capture your stone plus all stones from the opposite pit!

🏁 5. Endgame Sweep & Win

When either player clears all 6 of their pits, the game ends. The player who cleared their pits collects all remaining stones from the opponent's side. The player with the most stones in their treasury wins!
